## Information entered in onboarding

The screens request a phone number, name, date of birth and email address. Identity verification screens request BVN and NIN. This frontend sends OTP requests and identity numbers to the API configured for the app. The final controller, processors and lawful processing details are not identified in the handoff.

## Camera and biometric information

The selfie flow is intended to capture liveness information for identity matching. The handoff does not identify the verification provider, the data it receives, where it processes the data, retention/deletion periods, or the process for handling a failed match. Do not enable live capture/submission until these details and the approved notice are available.
