How long do interbank transfers take?

help_outline

Most interbank transfers settle instantly via NIBSS Instant Payments (NIP). In rare circumstances involving recipient bank network congestion, it may take up to 2 hours.

What to do if debit occurs without credit?

schedule Auto-reversal

If the beneficiary bank does not acknowledge within 24 hours, funds automatically revert to your balance as mandated by CBN auto-reversal standards. You can also file a ticket below with your Session ID.

Account Tiers & Limits

Tier 1 vs 2 vs 3 documentation, CBN rules

Tier 1 vs Tier 2 vs Tier 3 documentation

Tier 1 requires phone & BVN (₦50,000 daily transfer limit). Tier 2 adds verified NIN and ID (₦200,000 daily limit). Tier 3 unlocks unlimited transfers with utility bill address proof.

CBN compliance & KYC validation guidelines

Under Central Bank of Nigeria mandates, your full legal name on YourApp must strictly match the records held at the National Identity Management Commission (NIMC).
