How Pay Am Small Works

BUY SMART

Not an escrow account: Your dedicated savings vault safely locks the merchant’s sticker price and automatically settles the vendor when your goal is reached.

1

2

3
Vault pays vendor upon completion Once 100% funded, the vault disburses payment to the vendor. Receive delivery or pickup using your secret secure delivery code.
