# Master Design Brief (paste first)

**Product:** A mobile banking app for Nigeria. Naira only. It feels like a friendly, modern local bank: familiar to anyone who has used a Nigerian bank app, but warmer, calmer and more beautiful. Placeholder brand name: YourApp.

**Audience:** Everyday Nigerians (salary earners, students, traders, community and cooperative members), many on mid-range Android phones and slow networks. Many are not tech experts.

**Mood:** Calm, trustworthy, warm, premium-but-approachable. Generous whitespace, clear hierarchy, rounded shapes, soft shadows, one primary action per screen. Not flashy. No stock-photo clutter.

**Navigation:** Bottom bar with five tabs: Home, Payments, Cards, Save & Borrow, More. Common tasks within three taps.

**Visual language:**
- Rounded cards (16-24 px radius), soft elevation, subtle gradient on bank cards only.
- Primary colour deep berry; accent coral; warm neutral backgrounds. Exact values in the tokens file.
- Typeface: Inter (or a similar clean sans). Large bold numerals for amounts.
- Icons: simple outline icons, consistent stroke, 24 px.
- Status colours: success green, pending amber, error red, info blue. Brand colour never used for errors.

**Key components:** balance block with hide/show, quick action row, card carousel, transaction rows with status chips, bottom sheets for confirmations, custom PIN pad, shareable receipt.

**Rules:** No real bank logos or names. Keep text short and plain. Show fees and status clearly. Include accessible contrast and large tap targets (48 px). Design both empty and error states when asked.

**Deliverable per prompt:** one mobile screen at 390 x 844 in light mode, consistent with all previous screens.
