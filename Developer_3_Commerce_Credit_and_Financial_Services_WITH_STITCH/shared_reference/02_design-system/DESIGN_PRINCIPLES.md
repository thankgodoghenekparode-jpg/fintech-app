# Design Principles

1. **Calm and clear.** Generous spacing, one primary action per screen, few colours.
2. **Familiar structure, original identity.** Navigation and flows follow banking conventions; visuals, wording and cards are our own. No Zenith logo, icons or exact colours.
3. **Status and cost are always visible.** Every money action shows fee, total and outcome. States: Pending, Successful, Failed, Reversed.
4. **Three taps or fewer** for common tasks from Home.
5. **Trust cues.** Show "Account held at [foundation bank]" on account details and onboarding; show verification status; show who you are paying before confirmation.
6. **Beautiful but light.** Subtle gradients on cards only; limited animation; works on low-end Android phones.
7. **Friendly voice.** Plain English, short sentences, no jargon, no blame in errors.
8. **Accessible by default.** Contrast AA, text scales to 200%, screen reader labels, no information by colour alone.
9. **Built to grow.** Components are generic (amount, status chip, list row, sheet) so new products reuse them; currency is a parameter (v2 dollars).
10. **Brand colour never signals error.**
